# Branding

The product's identity marks — how a bot, a user or a space is recognised at a glance. Every mark derives deterministically from its seed so the same identity always renders the same.

## Components

- **AppIconMark** — the app icon, its rabbit alive inside the ground.
- **AvatarGroup** — several identities stacked in one row.
- **BotBadge** — the marks a bot wears: its dot, its mission strip, its title pill.
- **Bot Avatar** — the generated animal-and-blot mark, kept for the app icon.
- **BotIdentityAvatar** — the bot mark (its uploaded picture, else its dithered field) with its activity and connection state.
- **SpaceTint** — the per-space accent applied to a subtree.
- **DitheredFieldAvatar** — the companion avatar inside BotIdentityAvatar: a dithered field in the companion's hue, its silhouette and its screen (weight, halftone, ordered or organic) drawn from its name. **AsciiGlyphAvatar** and **DotLatticeAvatar** stay as retained explorations.

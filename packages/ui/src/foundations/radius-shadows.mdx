# Radius & Shadows

## Radius

One source value drives the whole scale:

```css
--radius: 0.625rem;
--radius-root: var(--radius);
```

The `@theme inline` block derives every step from `--radius-root`, which is frozen at the root: a subtree that redeclares `--radius` cannot reshape the scale under it, which is what the vendored sidebar does to its own header and content.

`xl` and `2xl` both resolve to the `lg` step. Every surface the reader meets — a menu, a button, a card, a sidebar row, a panel — carries the same corner, and reaching for a rounder step does not get you one. `xs`, `sm` and `md` stay tighter for marks, strips and inline code, `3xl` and `4xl` stay rounder for the composer, and `bubble` holds the message bubble's own step.

## Shadows

**`globals.css` declares no shadow tokens.** The system uses the Tailwind v4 default elevation scale as-is. Introducing custom shadow tokens is a design decision, not a story author's call.

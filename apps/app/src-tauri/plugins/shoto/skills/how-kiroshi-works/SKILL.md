Answers of one or two lines, one subject per answer: spaces (Personal and Work keep contexts apart, a companion can live in several), skills and History (what you tell a companion becomes a skill it keeps, readable and undoable in History), routines (a trigger runs a companion in a conversation, report lands there), missions (a piece of work a companion tracks with its state, visible in the panel and the roster), mentions (a companion hears another only through a mention in a conversation both sit in).

Never describe internal machinery (plugins, sessions, files).
